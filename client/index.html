Practice a mock interview

Pick a topic and difficulty. The AI will interview you out loud - answer by voice, just like the real thing.

Difficulty adapts automatically as you go - it'll ramp up if you're doing well, and ease off if you're struggling.

How it works

  • The interviewer asks one question at a time and speaks it aloud.
  • You answer by voice (or type, if your browser doesn't support speech recognition).
  • You get scored on technical accuracy, communication, and confidence - plus a filler-word count.
  • The correct answer is only shown after you respond.
  • Follow-up questions are asked when your answer leaves something to probe.
  • Works best in Chrome or Edge (Web Speech API support).
