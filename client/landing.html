Questions, answered

Do I need a microphone?

Voice is the best way to practice, and it works in Chrome and Edge. In any other browser you can type your answers instead.

How is the code graded?

Your solution is executed against test cases on our servers and you see which pass and fail - it isn't an AI guessing whether it looks right.

Which roles does it cover?

Data Scientist, ML Engineer and AI Engineer tracks, from Python and SQL fundamentals through deep learning, RAG and LLMOps.

Is my data private?

Your progress, resume and sessions are scoped to your account. Only the text needed for evaluation (your answers, code, resume text) is sent to the AI provider.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. No contracts - cancel from your account whenever you like.
