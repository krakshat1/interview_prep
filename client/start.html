The honest version of what this is

You don't get hired by "knowing AI" in some general sense - you get hired by clearing a specific sequence of interview rounds. Most self-taught people fail one of them not because they're not smart enough, but because they never practiced that specific round before the real interview. Each part of this tool exists to give you a low-stakes place to practice one of those rounds, and to actually measure whether you're improving instead of just guessing.

What each part is actually for

Roadmap Removes decision fatigue You don't have to figure out what to study today - it plans your days for you, in order.
Mock Interview The technical/verbal round Practices the actual moment of being asked a question out loud and having to answer under a little pressure, before that happens for real.
Code & SQL The technical coding round Graded by actually running your code - so "I think this works" turns into "I know this works."
System Design The design round The round most self-taught people are weakest at, simply because they've never had a reason to do it before.
Papers Vocabulary & concepts For when you don't even know the words yet - plain-English first, technical detail only if you want it.
Dashboard Reality check Real proof of progress, measured from what you've actually done - not a vague feeling of "do I know this or not."
Resume / Job Match / Applications The actual job hunt Defends the claims on your resume, checks you against real job descriptions, and tracks where you've applied.

Zero motivation today? Don't aim for "study AI." Aim for this.

Motivation usually shows up after you start, not before. So skip deciding - just do one small, low-stakes thing right now:

Completely lost on where to even start?

  1. Set up your roadmap (about 2 minutes) - tell it your target role and how much time you have per day.
  2. Do today's plan item - it tells you exactly one thing to do next, instead of an overwhelming list.
  3. Check your Dashboard once a week, not every day - that's often enough to see real movement and stay motivated by actual evidence.

You don't need to feel ready or motivated before you start - the practice is what creates both of those, not the other way around. You don't need to beat anyone else, either; the Dashboard only ever compares you to your own past attempts. Do one question today. That's the whole plan for right now.
